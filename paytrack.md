    description: |
      It provides a centralized platform where administrators and HR personnel can efficiently manage employee information, track daily attendance, calculate salaries, monitor payroll cutoffs, and maintain organized payroll records.

      The system is designed to reduce manual work, minimize calculation errors, and improve the accuracy and efficiency of payroll operations. Through its user-friendly dashboard, users can easily navigate different modules and access important payroll information.
			
      Key Features

						Managing employee records, monitoring attendance, and processing payroll manually can be time-consuming and prone to human error. Businesses need a centralized system to organize employee information, track working hours, and calculate salaries accurately.

      PayTrack addresses these challenges by providing an integrated platform for employee administration, attendance monitoring, and payroll processing.

      ## Key Features

      ### 1. Dashboard
      Provides an overview of the company's payroll activities, including employee statistics, attendance summaries, payroll information, and important system updates.

      ### 2. Employee Management
      Allows administrators to add, view, edit, search, and manage employee information, including personal details, department assignments, employment status, and salary information.

      ### 3. Attendance Management
      Enables users to monitor employee attendance, including time-in, time-out, total working hours, late arrivals, absences, and leave records. Attendance records can be filtered according to payroll cutoff periods.

      ### 4. Payroll Management
      Handles employee salary computation based on basic salary, overtime, allowances, and applicable deductions. It supports semi-monthly payroll periods, such as the 1st–15th and 16th–end of the month.

      ### 5. Reports
      Provides attendance and payroll reports that help administrators review employee attendance, salary computations, deductions, and payroll summaries. Reports can also be exported for documentation and recordkeeping.

      ### 6. Settings
      Allows administrators to manage company information, payroll configurations, attendance rules, account details, and system preferences.

      ### 7. User Authentication
      Provides a login interface for authorized users to access the system and manage payroll information.

      ## Project Objectives

      - Develop a centralized platform for managing employee and payroll information.
      - Automate and simplify employee attendance monitoring and payroll calculations.
      - Improve the accuracy and efficiency of salary computation.
      - Organize payroll processing through configurable cutoff periods.
      - Provide accessible attendance and payroll reports.
      - Reduce paperwork and manual administrative tasks.
      - Maintain organized and reliable employee and payroll records.

      ## Target Users

      PayTrack is intended for small to medium-sized businesses, company administrators, HR personnel, and payroll officers who need an organized and accessible way to manage employee information, attendance, and payroll operations.

      ## Expected Outcome

      PayTrack aims to provide a functional and user-friendly payroll management platform that makes employee administration, attendance tracking, and payroll processing more efficient, accurate, and manageable.
